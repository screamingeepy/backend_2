Moderators

Give a player Moderator access, or take it away. Administrators can't be changed here. A new Moderator can use the GM Portal straight away, and needs to log out and back in for the in-game commands.

    Maintenance & Player Lockdowns

    Block specific website and game actions for regular players when performing maintenance, updates, or testing. Staff accounts (Moderators and Administrators) can always bypass these lockdowns to test and moderate.

    Lock Player Logins …

    Temporarily blocks regular player logins to both the Flash game client and the website with a maintenance notice. Staff can still log in normally.

    Lock Account Registrations …

    Temporarily closes new account sign-ups on the website and Flash create-an-account screens.

    Lock Contact Tickets …

    Temporarily disables player contact inquiries and help desk ticket submissions.

    Lock Team Applications …

    Closes the Mod Team and Code Team application page. Turn it back on when you open applications.

    Lock Community Submissions …

    Temporarily pauses fairy tales, poems, questions for Tabby, and art submissions on Pixie Postings.

    Lock Contest Entries …

    Temporarily pauses all player entries across all active contests.
